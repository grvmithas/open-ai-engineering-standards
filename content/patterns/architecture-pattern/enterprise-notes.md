# Architecture: Enterprise Operations

> **Version:** 2.0 | **Updated:** 2026-07-16 | **Status:** Stable

## Purpose

Architecture enterprise-notes operationalizes this pattern outcome: choose and govern a system design against quantified quality-attribute scenarios.

## Tailored controls

Architecture review includes data residency, accessibility, procurement, concentration risk, cryptographic standards, support ownership, and decommission funding. Exceptions have expiry and accountable executives.

## Governance model

Architecture enterprise-notes governance binds owner, policy revision, and retained proof to "all priority scenarios have measurable responses", with "Supersede the ADR, disable the new topology at its seam, and restore the prior approved architecture" as the recorded recovery authority.

## Metrics and assurance

- Control dashboard reports all priority scenarios have measurable responses.
- Control dashboard reports prototype validates highest-risk assumption.
- Control dashboard reports fitness functions run in CI or operations.
- Control dashboard reports ADR review date and owner are current.

Architecture enterprise-notes metrics and assurance gate 3 is accepted only when "all priority scenarios have measurable responses" is source-verifiable; otherwise, supersede the adr, disable the new topology at its seam, and restore the prior approved architecture.

## Tradeoffs

Architecture enterprise-notes deliberately spends additional review time to protect "all priority scenarios have measurable responses"; evidence weakness triggers "Supersede the ADR, disable the new topology at its seam, and restore the prior approved architecture".

## Anti-patterns

Architecture enterprise-notes rejects shortcuts around "Name stakeholders and express quality attributes as stimulus-response scenarios." because they sever the evidence chain required for "all priority scenarios have measurable responses".
